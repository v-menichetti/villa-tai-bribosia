# Photo provenance

House and Grasse photographs: extracted from the owner's updated `Villa Tai Bribosia, au calme avec piscine 2027 (1).pptx`, supplied for this project. Originals are retained outside this repository. Web versions are resized without enlargement, converted to WebP and stripped of metadata. Object-fit crops are display-only. No generated house imagery is used.

Gourdon: MOSSOT, Gourdon (Alpes-Maritimes) -343.jpg, 6 June 2011. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gourdon_(Alpes-Maritimes)_-343.jpg . Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Resized, converted to WebP and cropped at display. Attribution and licence are also linked on the public privacy/credits and surroundings pages.

The unlicensed tourism images used in earlier mockups are not included. The coast section uses an illustration and a link to the tourism office. Generated mockup files under docs/design are design references only.

## November visual refinement

Built-in ImageGen created three project assets: `olive-footer.webp` (dark low-contrast olive branches, panoramic, quiet centre), `napoule-generated.webp` (summer editorial illustration of the seaside castle), and `gourdon-generated.webp` (warm panoramic hilltop village illustration). These are AI illustrations, not documentary photographs; the public credits disclose this. The former Gourdon photo remains archived with its original attribution in this file but is no longer displayed.
